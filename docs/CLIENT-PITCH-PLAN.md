# Koushik's client pitch and delivery plan

## The offer

“I build websites for local service businesses that make your work easy to understand, help customers explore their options and give them a clear way to enquire.”

Use the Cedar & Stone demo as an example of what is possible. It is a fictional concept, so introduce it as a sample rather than a previous client result. Avoid promises about guaranteed leads, search rankings or revenue.

## The two-minute demo

| Time | Show | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:20 | Hero on desktop, then the mobile layout | “This gives someone discovering your business a clear first impression and one obvious next step.” |
| 0:20–0:45 | Move the trowel, then scroll through the wall layers | “Instead of just listing services, we show what goes into the work. We can design a relevant interaction for your trade.” |
| 0:45–1:10 | Scroll the material track, open a sample, choose it | “Customers can explore finishes before the first call, and their preference carries into the project brief.” |
| 1:10–1:30 | Scroll through the service-area measuring tape | “We can make your service area memorable too. Your version would use verified locations and coverage.” |
| 1:30–2:00 | Complete the brief with dummy data, download it | “This demo builds a brief locally. For your website, we can connect an agreed delivery method and test that enquiries reach the right person.” |

Close with: “Which service do you most want to showcase, and where should a new enquiry go?”

## Start with one niche

1. Start with exterior contractors or painters, because this demo is immediately relevant.
2. Build a shortlist of 15–20 businesses through public listings and their own sites. Prioritise those with active work photos but unclear service information or a difficult enquiry journey.
3. Write down one specific observation for each business. Avoid generic statements such as “your website is bad.”
4. Personalise the demo hero and one service section after learning what the business needs. Use its logo/photos only with permission.
5. Share a short screen recording and the live demo link. Follow up once after a few working days if appropriate. This plan does not send messages for you.

## Outreach draft

> Hi [Name], I noticed [one specific detail about their work]. I’m Koushik, and I build websites for local service businesses. I made a sample contractor site where customers can explore finishes and put together a clear project brief. A similar approach could help you showcase [their service] and make enquiries easier. Here’s the demo: [your GitHub Pages link]. Would a short walkthrough be useful?

## Discovery before quoting

- Which service, location and customer type should the site prioritise?
- What should a visitor do: call, message, request a site visit or submit a brief?
- Who responds, and how quickly can they reasonably respond?
- Do they own a domain, logo and approved photographs? Who supplies and approves the text?
- Which claims can they substantiate: qualifications, reviews, warranties, project descriptions and service area?
- Do they need multiple languages, a content editor, payments, scheduling or integrations? Scope these separately.
- What are the available budget, decision process and launch constraints?

## Example packages to adapt

These are **proposed starting quotes in INR for your offer**, not researched market rates or revenue forecasts. Adjust them to your effort, client geography and operating costs. Quote taxes, domains, paid subscriptions, additional content, photography and continuing support separately as applicable.

| Package | Proposed starting quote | Defined deliverable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Essential | ₹15,000 | One responsive landing page: services, approved gallery, FAQs, contact CTA, metadata, deployment and one revision round. A contact CTA can be a verified phone/email link; custom form delivery is extra. |
| Signature | ₹30,000 | Essential scope plus one custom interaction, a material/service catalogue, and an enquiry flow connected to one agreed provider. Two revision rounds and a short handover. |
| Connected | ₹50,000+ | A separately estimated scope for multiple pages, editable content or one business integration, with acceptance criteria and a defined support period. |

Price complex animation, multilingual content, CRM/booking work and ongoing changes against the actual scope. Offer maintenance only when you can state its response time, included hours and exclusions. Optional payment milestones: 50% to schedule, 30% after design approval and 20% after the agreed launch checks. Put payment terms and revision limits in the written agreement.

## A practical 7-working-day delivery outline

The schedule starts after the client supplies the agreed content and access. Allow more time for integrations or additional review rounds.

## What to customise for the first client

Replace Cedar & Stone with the approved business identity, use its own service language and photos, add accurate coverage/contact information, and choose a relevant interaction. A painter might compare colours; a roofer might explore roof layers; a flooring installer might filter finishes. Confirm all product claims with the business.

For a working lead form, agree whether to use a hosted form endpoint or a small backend. Add error handling, spam controls, appropriate consent/privacy information and a test that confirms delivery. GitHub Pages can host the frontend but does not run server-side application code. [GitHub Pages documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/creating-a-github-pages-site).

## Publish and build evidence

Deploy this demo with the steps in the [README](../README.md). Record a 60–90 second desktop/mobile walkthrough. After a real launch, measure agreed enquiry events and use actual results to improve your offer.
