# Cedar & Stone — composition demo

## Aim

Build a fresh demonstration of the reel's structure and physical material presentation. The earlier site remains at the repository root; the new version lives at `/demo/` for comparison. The reference is the saved 17.7-second Instagram reel DendM5Rjk3Y, reviewed again at closely spaced timestamps on 20 October 2026. This is an original implementation, not the reference site's source.

## What changes

| Area | New composition | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Header | Compact navy strip, small wordmark, quiet navigation, yellow project action | Preserve the canvas for the craft demonstration |
| Opening | Top-aligned editorial headline and short side copy over a fine metal-lath wall | Match the reference's hierarchy and keep the material visible |
| Wall | Independent pointer plaster; three rough-edged coats driven by scroll; four compact stage controls below | Explain the assembly through a coherent physical sequence |
| Final coat | Texture and colour choices placed directly on the full wall | Show a finish at architectural scale |
| Catalogue | One continuous rail with an intro followed by twelve tall material boards, narrow gaps and compact cream captions | Let surfaces dominate; avoid a dashboard appearance |
| Services | Six original technical illustrations on ivory | Give the visitor a short, clear vertical passage |
| Process | Four wide numbered rows on blue, fills advancing on entry | Explain the order of a project without repeated cards |
| Service area | Cream scene; tape housing, extending blade, end hook, ticks and alternating labels in one coordinate system | Make distance and movement read as a physical tape |
| Project | One compact form, selected finish summary, downloadable/copyable brief | End with a useful demo action and clear local-only behaviour |

## Visual system

- Deep navy `#172f3e`: navigation and opening wall.
- Slate blue `#2b5066`: material library, process and project section.
- Warm ivory `#f4efdf`: service area, captions and services.
- Saffron `#f3cd62`: actions, progress and measuring tape.
- Light text `#fcf8ef`; navy text on ivory; muted text remains readable.
- Substantial serif headings with a plain sans-serif for controls/captions. Shared type sizes, section margins and divider rules.
- Original procedural material textures with joints, grain, bevels and fine surface variation. No reference frames are shipped.

## Behaviour and acceptance

1. Three scroll scenes remain reversible and release at their ends. Navigation reaches scene entrances.
2. Pointer plaster does not advance the wall. A button provides a touch/keyboard alternative.
3. Stage buttons, finish choices, material navigation, filters and dialogs work. A saved finish reaches the form.
4. The tape starts retracted, extends, carries its marks/labels, reaches the final distance and retracts on reverse scroll. Range/keyboard alternatives remain available.
5. Mobile layouts keep the visual sequence and usable controls. Reduced motion and short landscapes use ordinary scrolling and manual exploration.
6. The form validates input, safely builds a local brief and provides actual copy/download/reset actions. No enquiry is reported sent.
7. Check the opening, transitions, final boards, tape endpoints, modal focus, page overflow, images/fonts and runtime errors in a real browser session before publishing.
8. Verify GitHub Pages serves the committed new demo files, then repeat the important interactions on the live URL.

Cedar & Stone, sample names, places and distances are illustrative. No real contractor credentials, reviews or completed projects are claimed.
