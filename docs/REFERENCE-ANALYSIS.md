## Interpretation for your offer

The repeatable opportunity is a trade-specific website that explains the craft, helps homeowners choose materials and gives them a clear route to discuss a project. The visual interactions make the site easier to remember during a sales conversation. Their effect on actual enquiries would need to be measured after launch.

Good initial prospects: exterior contractors, painters, roofing firms, flooring installers, landscapers and similar visual service businesses. Pick a niche where you can obtain approved photographs and explain the work accurately. Adapt the core interaction to that trade rather than changing only the logo.

## What this demo implements

- Original **Cedar & Stone** identity with forest green, cream, warm clay accents and editorial typography.
- A slider/button-driven wall build-up as an accessible alternative to the reference's trowel interaction. The scratch/brown coats are simplified into a base-coat stage; it is an illustration, not installation guidance.
- Six procedural material samples with filters, detail dialogs and selection into a project brief.
- Three clearly labelled inspiration concepts using stock photographs.
- A guided brief visitors can download or copy, plus an explanation of the project process and FAQs.

The reference's tape-measure animation is not reproduced. No claim is made about its build tools, build time, revenue, conversion performance or the authenticity of its displayed contractor business.
