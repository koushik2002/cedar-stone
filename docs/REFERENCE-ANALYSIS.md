# Cedar & Stone — second review of the reference reel

Source: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DendM5Rjk3Y/

Reviewed on 20 October 2026 using the saved 17.7-second video of this exact reel. Exact-time frames were extracted at closer intervals around the transitions. The reel demonstrates Lath & Lap Exteriors on a desktop screen. These notes describe the recorded appearance; the original website's source code and mobile behaviour were not inspected. Timestamps below refer to the recording, not fixed animation durations for a new website.

## 1. Opening wall scene, approximately 0–3.5 seconds

- The material wall occupies the main opening scene. Headline and action sit over the mesh; a four-stage explanation remains along the bottom.
- The opening instruction distinguishes moving the pointer to trowel from scrolling through the coats. These should be treated as separate interactions.
- As coat progress starts, the headline and action fade out. The header and stage explanation remain in place while the wall changes.
- Coatings move from left to right through irregular masks. Grooves, rough borders, edge shadows and surface grain provide the material effect.
- An advancing line and the emphasis of the stage labels track progress.
- At the completed wall, texture and colour controls become visible. The recording shows the controls, but does not demonstrate clicking through every option.

## 2. Material track, approximately 3.5–11.2 seconds

- The section enters with a large introductory text panel, followed by tall sample boards.
- The text panel and sample boards belong to the same sideways-moving track. The introduction exits to the left as materials enter from the right.
- During the traverse, the section stays beneath the header. After the final samples, vertical page movement resumes into services.
- The visible sequence contains eleven finishes: four horizontal siding treatments, two vertical panel treatments, two shingle treatments and three plaster treatments.
- Each card has a large textured surface, a cream information panel and compact suitability text. Grain, joints, overlaps and shadows distinguish the material types.
- My implementation reading is a pinned section mapping vertical scroll progress to horizontal track translation. The reel does not establish the actual library or code used.

## 3. Services and process, approximately 11.2–13.8 seconds

- Six service entries use restrained technical line illustrations in a desktop grid.
- The process section contains four wide numbered rows. Background fills advance across the rows as they come into view.
- These sections provide shorter vertical passages between the longer material and tape scenes.

## 4. Measuring tape, approximately 13.8–16.8 seconds

- A yellow tape housing appears first, with the blade initially retracted.
- The blade extends to the right with a visible metal end hook. The housing subsequently travels out of view to the left.
- Continued scrolling advances the distance graduations and associated locations together across the scene. The heading and explanatory copy remain in place during the horizontal journey.
- Locations alternate above and below the blade. Incoming labels become more legible as the track progresses.
- The blade has major and minor ticks, numbered distances, shading and a cast shadow. Its movement has a physical tape-measure appearance.
- At the end of the distance journey, the scene releases and moves vertically into the final contact section.
- This represents service coverage by distance. It is not shown as a measurement input or product-sizing calculator.

## 5. Overall presentation

The reference uses navy, warm cream and a yellow accent, large serif headings, thin dividers, persistent navigation and material surfaces as its main visual content. A compact contact section closes the page. Form submission and delivery are not demonstrated in the recording.

## Cedar & Stone adaptation, 20 October 2026

The site now implements the three central interaction ideas using original HTML, CSS, SVG and canvas code:

- A pinned opening wall, with independent pointer troweling, rough-edged scroll coatings, four stage labels and a full-wall texture/colour picker.
- One continuous material track containing an introductory panel and twelve original sample boards, including an additional stone finish. Filters, dialogs and selection into the local project brief remain available.
- A pinned yellow tape with a case, extending blade, metal hook, major/minor graduations and alternating place labels. The illustrative area spans 0–40 km; its seven places are fictional, not verified service coverage.
- Six line-art services and four numbered process rows with fills linked to viewport entry.

Cedar & Stone keeps its own forest-green identity, typography, copy and textures. The reference video and screenshots are not bundled. This is an adaptation of observed interaction ideas, not the reference website's source or an assertion of an exact reproduction.

Keyboard buttons and range controls offer alternatives to scrolling and pointer input. The material track uses native horizontal swiping on mobile. Reduced-motion preferences and short viewports disable pinned scenes; all exploration remains available through ordinary scrolling and manual controls.

The guided project brief is a local demo with download/copy actions. It does not send enquiries. Validation evidence and browser limitations are recorded in [QA.md](QA.md).
