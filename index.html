A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Let’s clear
things up.

Good decisions start with a little clarity.

How do I choose the right material?+

Start with the look you like, then discuss your home’s existing walls, exposure, maintenance preferences and budget with your contractor. The samples here are a starting point for that conversation.

Can I combine different finishes?+

Yes, a design can combine siding, stucco and stone accents. The detailing and transitions need to suit the selected products and wall assembly.

What goes into an exterior quote?+

Wall area, material choices, access, removal of the existing finish, repairs and detailing all affect the scope. A contractor should assess the site before confirming a price.
